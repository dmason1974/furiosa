## 🛡️ Moderator Authority

- Game moderators must be **listened to, respected, and followed** when issuing in-game instructions.
- This applies **even if you believe the moderator is mistaken at the time**.
- **Instructions must be followed first** — disputes are handled afterwards.
- Intentional disrespect, insults, or misconduct directed at a moderator may result in:
  - Disqualification of an individual player
  - In extreme cases, disqualification of the entire team

These principles **apply consistently across all Fury Road events** and persist regardless of event format, ruleset, or map.

---

## 📜 Rules

- By logging in and participating in a game map, you agree to follow **all rules associated with that event**.
- It is **your responsibility** to read and understand the rules before playing.
- Any rule violation will be handled by the moderator, and **all players must comply with moderator decisions**.
- Failure to comply with rule enforcement may result in **disqualification**.
