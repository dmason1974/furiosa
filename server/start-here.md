# Start here 👋
Welcome to **Fury Road**. We run organised themed events on Supremacy World War 3 maps. New players are welcome, and you don't need a team to get started. Want the full picture of what we're about? See {{🖋️server-description🖋️}}.

## How an event works
**1. Announced.** Every event gets its own post in {{upcoming-event}} with the format, team size, dates and how to sign up. Key dates also appear under **Events** at the top of the channel list, shown in your own time zone.
**2. Sign up.** Follow the sign-up steps in that event's post before sign-ups close. Clicking *Interested* on a Discord Event sets a reminder. **It does not sign you up.**
**3. Teams and starts.** Once sign-ups close, staff confirm the teams and draw who starts where.
**4. Your team thread.** For each event your team gets a private thread with your countries and rules. Tick ✅ on the first message to confirm you've read it.
**5. Play.** The map opens at the posted time. Raise disputes in a support ticket, not in the event chat.
