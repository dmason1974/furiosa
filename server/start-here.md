# Start here 👋
Welcome to **Fury Road**. We run organised themed events on Supremacy World War 3 maps. New players are welcome, and you don't need a team to get started. Want the full picture of what we're about? See {{🖋️server-description🖋️}}.

## How an event works
**1. Announced.** Every event gets its own post in {{upcoming-event}} with the format, team size, dates and how to sign up.

**2. Show interest.** Find the event under **Events** at the top of the channel list (times are shown in your own time zone) and click *Interested*. You'll be kept informed of updates to that event. **This does not sign you up.**

**3. Sign up.** Once registration for an event opens, open its **#registration** channel and run:
`/register team:<team name> ign:<in-game name>`
Everyone on a team uses the same team name.

No team? Post in the event's **#looking-for-team** channel.
To withdraw, run:
`/unregister`

**4. Teams and starts.** Approved teams appear in **#registered-teams**. Once sign-ups close, staff confirm the teams. Zone draws take place as live events in {{event draw}}.

**5. Your team thread.** For each event your team gets a private thread with your countries and rules. Tick ✅ on the first message to confirm you've read it.

**6. Play.** The map opens at the posted time. Raise disputes in a support ticket, not in the event chat.
