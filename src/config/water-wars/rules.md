### 🌊 Water Wars: Event Rules
*These rules apply to every round of this event. They sit on top of the server rules and the [definitions and sanctions](<https://dmason1974.github.io/furiosa/definitions.html>).*

### ⚙️ Format
```ansi
{blue}Teams       {/} : 16 teams of 5 players
{blue}Map         {/} : WW3 4x
{blue}Round 1     {/} : 2 maps of 8 teams
{blue}Round 2     {/} : 1 map of 8 teams
{blue}Final       {/} : 1 map of 4 teams, played across the whole ocean
{blue}Free-for-all{/} : On the Round 1 and Round 2 maps, 4 teams share
               each ocean, the Pacific Theatre and the Indo-
               Atlantic Theatre
```

<!-- split -->

### 🏆 Win Condition
```ansi
{blue}Flagship    {/} : Capture the Flagship: a team is out as soon as
               its flagship is destroyed
{blue}Advancing   {/} : The last 2 flagships standing in each ocean
               advance
{blue}Final       {/} : The last flagship standing wins the event
{blue}Early finish{/} : When only 2 flagships remain in an ocean, that
               ocean's round is over
{blue}Tiebreaker  {/} : If more than 2 flagships are still alive after 8
               hours, the surviving teams are ranked by enemy
               ships sunk, weighted by ship class. The top 2
               advance. A team that avoids fighting scores
               nothing
```
*The ship class weights and how kills are counted are still to be confirmed.*

### 📅 Schedule
```ansi
{blue}Registration{/} : To be confirmed
{blue}Draw        {/} : A live draw assigns every team to a map and a
               zone. Date to be confirmed
{blue}Map start   {/} : The map opens on a Sunday and the 6-day truce
               begins
{blue}War start   {/} : The following Saturday, for 8 hours
```
*Each round follows the same pattern: Round 1 on both maps together, Round 2 after the Round 1 results, and the final after Round 2. The start time is to be confirmed (previous events used 14:00 UTC).*

### 📜 Rules
🚢 Your flagship
```ansi
{blue}Position{/} : At sea inside your theatre
{blue}Unit    {/} : One Naval Veteran named after your team. It
           cannot be changed
```
✅ Allowed
```ansi
{green}Air        {/} : Naval Patrol Aircraft and AWACS
{green}Missiles   {/} : Ballistic and cruise
{green}Officer    {/} : Uncommon Infantry Officer
{green}Reinforcing{/} : Ships may move into your theatre before war
              starts and during the round
{green}Targets    {/} : Ships anywhere at sea, including territorial
              waters, and in port
{green}Units      {/} : Seasonal units
```
⛔ Not allowed
```ansi
{red}All event  {/} : Conspiracy, espionage, gold usage, market
              blocking, restricted unit usage
{red}Capturing  {/} : Any country outside your homeland, including
              admin countries
{red}Fighting   {/} : Outside your theatre
{red}Flagship in{/} : Baltic Sea, Black Sea, Caspian Sea, Great Lakes,
              Persian Gulf, Red Sea, and all rivers and lakes
{red}Land       {/} : Bombarding or taking another player's cities,
              ports or provinces. Exception: a city with
              hostile units in it may be attacked
{red}Officers   {/} : All except the Uncommon Infantry Officer
{red}Truce      {/} : Act of war, failing to make peace, flyover,
              mines, nation's territory, territorial waters,
              unjustified engagement
{red}Units      {/} : AIP submarines, chemical warheads, ICBM
              missiles, nuclear warheads, satellites
```
