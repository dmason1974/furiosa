# 🌊 Water Wars: Event Rules
*These rules apply to every round of this event. They sit on top of the server rules and the definitions in this channel.*

## ⚙️ Format
🔷 **16 teams** of 5 players, on the WW3 4x map
🔷 **Round 1:** 2 maps of 8 teams
🔷 **Round 2:** 1 map of 8 teams
🔷 **Final:** 1 map of 4 teams, played across the whole ocean
🔷 **Free-for-all:** on the Round 1 and Round 2 maps, 4 teams share each ocean, the **Pacific Theatre** and the **Indo-Atlantic Theatre**

<!-- split -->

## 🏆 Win Condition
🔷 **Capture the Flagship:** a team is out as soon as its flagship is destroyed
🔷 **Advancing:** the last **2 flagships** standing in each ocean advance
🔷 **Final:** the last flagship standing wins the event
🔷 **Early finish:** when only **2 flagships** remain in an ocean, that ocean's round is over
🔷 **Tiebreaker:** if more than 2 flagships are still alive after 8 hours, the surviving teams are ranked by **enemy ships sunk**, weighted by ship class. The top 2 advance. A team that avoids fighting scores nothing
*The ship class weights and how kills are counted are still to be confirmed.*

## 🎁 Prizes
🔷 Prizes are paid in **gold and SC** only. Officers cannot be given as a prize

## 📅 Schedule
🔷 **Registration:** to be confirmed
🔷 **Draw:** a live draw assigns every team to a map and a zone. Date to be confirmed
🔷 **Map start (truce):** the map opens on a **Sunday** and the **6-day truce** begins
🔷 **War start:** the following **Saturday**, for **8 hours**
*Each round follows the same pattern: Round 1 on both maps together, Round 2 after the Round 1 results, and the final after Round 2. The start time is to be confirmed (previous events used 14:00 UTC).*

## 📜 Rules
🔶 **No bombardment of cities.** Ships may not bombard another player's cities
🔶 **No invading provinces.** Do not take any province belonging to another player
🔶 **Capture only your own homeland countries.** All other countries, including admin countries, must not be captured
🔷 **The sea is open.** Ships can be attacked anywhere at sea, including territorial waters, and in port. The port itself cannot be attacked
🔷 **Your flagship:** one Naval Veteran named after your team. It cannot be changed and must stay at sea inside your theatre
🔶 **Flagships are out of bounds in:** Caspian Sea, Black Sea, Great Lakes, Baltic Sea, Persian Gulf, Red Sea, and all rivers and lakes
🔷 **Reinforcements:** ships may move into your theatre before war starts and during the round. No fighting outside your theatre
🔶 **Banned:** satellites, AIP submarines, ICBM missiles, chemical and nuclear warheads, and all officers except the Uncommon Infantry Officer
🔷 **Allowed:** Naval Patrol Aircraft and AWACS, ballistic and cruise missiles, seasonal units
🔶 **Peace-time:** no act of war and no mines
🔷 **Definitions and sanctions:** the full wording and the sanction for each are on the [definitions and sanctions page](https://dmason1974.github.io/furiosa/definitions.html). In Water Wars:
➢ **Enforced all event:** conspiracy, espionage, gold usage, market blocking, restricted unit usage
➢ **Enforced during the truce:** act of war, failing to make peace, unjustified engagement, flyover, nation's territory, territorial waters
➢ **Not used:** Homeland Supremacy, Sacred Ground
