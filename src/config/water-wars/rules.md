## 🌊 Water Wars: Event Rules
These rules apply to every round of this event. They sit on top of the server rules and the definitions in this channel.

### Format
```
Teams        16 teams of 5, 3 rounds, WW3 4x map
Oceans       4 teams share each ocean on every map:
             Pacific Theatre and Indo-Atlantic Theatre
Truce        6 days. The map opens on a Sunday and
             the war starts the following Saturday.
War length   8 hours per map
Win          Capture the Flagship. A team is out as soon
             as its flagship is destroyed.
Advancing    The last 2 flagships standing in each ocean
Final        One map, four teams. The last flagship
             standing wins.
```

## 🏁 How a round ends
```
Early finish  When only 2 flagships remain in an ocean,
              that ocean's round is over and both teams
              advance.

Time-up       If more than 2 flagships are still alive
              after 8 hours, the surviving teams are
              ranked by enemy ships sunk, weighted by
              ship class. The top 2 advance.

Hiding        A team that avoids fighting scores nothing,
              so it can be outranked even with its
              flagship alive.
```

## ⚓ Rules of Engagement
```
1. SEA ONLY BETWEEN PLAYERS
   - No bombardment of cities. Ships may not bombard
     a city belonging to another player.
   - No invading provinces. You may not invade or take
     any province belonging to another player.
   - Countries that are not your homeland in #zones must
     not be captured, including admin countries.

2. THE SEA IS OPEN
   Ships may be attacked anywhere at sea, including in
   territorial waters. Ships in port can be attacked.
   The port itself cannot.

3. YOUR FLAGSHIP
   - Name one Naval Veteran after your team. It must
     stay identifiable and cannot be changed.
   - It must stay at sea inside your theatre for the
     whole round.
   - Out of bounds for flagships: Caspian Sea, Black Sea,
     Great Lakes, Baltic Sea, Persian Gulf, Red Sea,
     and all rivers and lakes.

4. MOVING SHIPS IN
   Ships may be moved into your theatre before war
   starts and during the round to reinforce. Fighting
   outside your theatre is not allowed.
```

## 📚 Definitions & Sanctions
The full wording of every definition, and the sanction for each, is on the definitions and sanctions page: https://dmason1974.github.io/furiosa/definitions.html
This is which of them are enforced in Water Wars.
```
Term                    In Water Wars
Capture the Flagship    In use: the win condition
Homeland Supremacy      Not used
Sacred Ground           Not used

Conspiracy              Enforced
Espionage               Enforced
Gold usage              Enforced
Market blocking         Enforced
Restricted unit usage   Enforced (satellites, AIP)

Act of war              Enforced during the truce
Failing to make peace   Enforced during the truce
Unjustified engagement  Enforced during the truce
Flyover                 Enforced during the truce
Nation's territory      Applies during the truce
Territorial waters      Applies during the truce
```
### Sanctions
```
A breach of an enforced term costs the sanction listed
on the definitions page.
- Sanctions are paid to the event moderator.
- Until a sanction is paid, the team cannot conquer
  territory or mobilise more units, including during
  the truce.
- Refusing to pay a sanction means disqualification.
- Gold usage: disqualification unless self-reported.

The Rules of Engagement above have no sanction on that
page yet (see below).
```
