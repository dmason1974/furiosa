## 🌊 Water Wars: Event Rules
These rules apply to every round of this event. They sit on top of the server rules and the definitions in this channel.
### Format
```ansi
{blue}Teams{/}          16 teams of 5, 3 rounds, WW3 4x map
{blue}Oceans{/}         4 teams share each ocean on every map: Pacific Theatre and Indo-Atlantic Theatre
{blue}Truce{/}          {yellow}6 days{/}. The map opens on a Sunday and the war starts the following Saturday.
{blue}War length{/}     {yellow}8 hours{/} per map
{blue}Win{/}            Capture the Flagship. A team is out as soon as its flagship is destroyed.
{blue}Advancing{/}      The last {yellow}2 flagships{/} standing in each ocean advance.
{blue}Final{/}          One map, four teams. The last flagship standing wins.
```

## 🏁 How a round ends
```ansi
{blue}Early finish{/}   When only {yellow}2 flagships{/} remain in an ocean, that ocean's round is over and both advance.
{blue}Time-up{/}        If more than 2 flagships are still alive after 8 hours, the surviving teams are ranked by
               {yellow}enemy ships sunk{/}, weighted by ship class. The top 2 advance.
{blue}Hiding{/}         A team that avoids fighting scores nothing, even if its flagship is still alive.
```

<!-- split -->

## ⚓ Rules of Engagement
```ansi
{blue}1. Sea only between players{/}
   {red}No bombardment of cities.{/} Ships may not bombard a city belonging to another player.
   {red}No invading provinces.{/} You may not invade or take any province belonging to another player.
   Countries that are not your homeland in #zones must not be captured, including admin countries.

{blue}2. The sea is open{/}
   Ships may be attacked anywhere at sea, including in territorial waters.
   Ships in port can be attacked. The port itself cannot.

{blue}3. Your flagship{/}
   Name one Naval Veteran after your team. It must stay identifiable and cannot be changed.
   It must stay at sea inside your theatre for the whole round.
   {red}Out of bounds for flagships:{/} Caspian Sea, Black Sea, Great Lakes, Baltic Sea, Persian Gulf,
   Red Sea, and all rivers and lakes.

{blue}4. Moving ships in{/}
   Ships may be moved into your theatre before war starts and during the round to reinforce.
   Fighting outside your theatre is not allowed.
```

## 📚 Definitions
The full wording of every definition, and the sanction for each, is on the definitions and sanctions page: https://dmason1974.github.io/furiosa/definitions.html
This is which of them are enforced in Water Wars.
```ansi
{blue}Win conditions{/}
Capture the Flagship    {green}In use{/}                       
Homeland Supremacy      {gray}Not used{/}                     
Sacred Ground           {gray}Not used{/}                     

{blue}Enforced all event{/}                                   {blue}Sanction{/}

Espionage               {green}Enforced{/}                     1000 Components, 1000 Electronics
Gold usage              {green}Enforced{/}                     {red}Disqualification{/} unless self-reported
Market blocking         {green}Enforced{/}                     The value of the resources used to block

{blue}Truce only{/}                                           {blue}Sanction{/}
Act of war              {green}Enforced{/} {yellow}during the truce{/}    500 Electronics, 1000 Rare materials
Failing to make peace   {green}Enforced{/} {yellow}during the truce{/}    1000 Supplies, 500 Rare materials
Unjustified engagement  {green}Enforced{/} {yellow}during the truce{/}    1.5 x cost in all 6 resources per damaged unit

Nation's territory      Applies {yellow}during the truce{/}     Definition only
Territorial waters      Applies {yellow}during the truce{/}     Definition only
```

## 💰 Sanctions
```ansi
A breach of an enforced term costs the sanction in the table above (full wording: definitions page).

  Sanctions are paid to the event moderator. Until one is paid, the team cannot conquer territory
  or mobilise more units, including during the truce.
  {red}Refusing to pay a sanction means disqualification.{/}

The Rules of Engagement above have no sanction on that page yet (see the open items below).
```
