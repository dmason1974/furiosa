# 🌊 Water Wars: Event Rules
*These rules apply to every round of this event. They sit on top of the server rules and the definitions in this channel.*

## ⚙️ Format
🔷 16 teams of 5, 3 rounds, WW3 4x map
🔷 4 teams share each ocean on every map: **Pacific Theatre** and **Indo-Atlantic Theatre**
🔷 **Truce:** 6 days. The map opens on a Sunday and the war starts the following Saturday
🔷 **War length:** 8 hours per map
🔷 **Win condition:** Capture the Flagship. A team is out as soon as its flagship is destroyed
🔷 **Advancing:** the last **2 flagships** standing in each ocean advance
🔷 **Final:** one map, four teams. The last flagship standing wins

<!-- split -->

## 🏁 How a round ends
🔷 **Early finish:** when only **2 flagships** remain in an ocean, that ocean's round is over and both teams advance
🔷 **Time-up:** if more than 2 flagships are still alive after 8 hours, the surviving teams are ranked by **enemy ships sunk**, weighted by ship class. The top 2 advance
🔷 **Hiding:** a team that avoids fighting scores nothing, even if its flagship is still alive

<!-- split -->

## ⚓ Rules of Engagement
**Sea only between players**
🔶 **No bombardment of cities.** Ships may not bombard a city belonging to another player
🔶 **No invading provinces.** You may not invade or take any province belonging to another player
🔶 Countries that are not your homeland in #zones must not be captured, including admin countries

**The sea is open**
🔷 Ships may be attacked anywhere at sea, including in territorial waters
🔷 Ships in port can be attacked. The port itself cannot

**Your flagship**
🔷 Name one Naval Veteran after your team. It must stay identifiable and cannot be changed
🔷 It must stay at sea inside your theatre for the whole round
🔶 **Out of bounds for flagships:** Caspian Sea, Black Sea, Great Lakes, Baltic Sea, Persian Gulf, Red Sea, and all rivers and lakes

**Moving ships in**
🔷 Ships may be moved into your theatre before war starts and during the round to reinforce
🔶 Fighting outside your theatre is not allowed

## 🪖 Units & Settings
*Carried over from Beyond Thunderdome unless stated here. To be confirmed for this event.*
🔶 **Not allowed:** satellites, AIP submarines, ICBM missiles, chemical and nuclear warheads, and all officers except the Uncommon Infantry Officer
🔶 **Not allowed:** conspiracy, espionage, gold usage and market blocking
🔷 **Allowed:** Naval Patrol Aircraft and AWACS, ballistic and cruise missiles, the Uncommon Infantry Officer, and seasonal units

**Peace-time rules**
🔶 Any act of war is not allowed
🔶 Deploying mines is not allowed

## 📚 Definitions
*The full wording of every definition, and the sanction for each, is on the [definitions and sanctions page](https://dmason1974.github.io/furiosa/definitions.html). This is which of them are enforced in Water Wars.*

**Win conditions**
🔷 **Capture the Flagship:** in use
🔷 **Homeland Supremacy** and **Sacred Ground:** not used

**Enforced all event**
🔶 **Conspiracy:** 1000 Rare materials per team member
🔶 **Espionage:** 1000 Components, 1000 Electronics
🔶 **Gold usage:** disqualification unless self-reported
🔶 **Market blocking:** the value of the resources used to block the market
🔶 **Restricted unit usage:** the units are destroyed (satellites, AIP)

**Enforced during the truce only**
🔶 **Act of war:** 500 Electronics, 1000 Rare materials
🔶 **Failing to make peace:** 1000 Supplies, 500 Rare materials
🔶 **Unjustified engagement:** 1.5 × cost in all 6 resources, per damaged unit
🔶 **Flyover:** the same as an act of war
➢ **Nation's territory** and **Territorial waters** apply during the truce. They are definitions only, with no sanction of their own

<!-- split -->

## 💰 Sanctions
🔷 A breach of an enforced term costs the sanction listed above
🔷 Sanctions are paid to the event moderator
🔶 Until a sanction is paid, the team cannot conquer territory or mobilise more units, including during the truce
🔶 **Refusing to pay a sanction means disqualification**
*The Rules of Engagement above have no sanction on the definitions page yet (see the open items below).*
